# MBA presentation outline (10–12 minutes)

1. Business background and next-month default-risk problem.
2. Dataset source, Taiwan 2005 context, 30,000 rows and target.
3. Data quality: two-row header issue, missingness, duplicates and ID exclusion.
4. EDA: class distribution, PAY_0, credit-limit and age associations.
5. Feature preparation and leakage-aware pipeline.
6. Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest comparison.
7. Validation-based model selection.
8. Held-out test metrics and confusion-matrix business meaning.
9. Streamlit Business Overview, Data Insights and Prediction pages.
10. GitHub and cloud deployment (show live app only after testing).
11. Limitations: historical data, calibration, threshold economics and fairness.
12. Conclusion and recommendations.

Each group member should be ready to explain every stage. Do not make causal claims from observational patterns.
